# Code of Conduct

Sphere follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming, be kind, critique ideas rather than people, and assume good faith. Harassment and personal
attacks aren't tolerated.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately through the repository's
[security advisories](https://github.com/xiaojia630/sphere/security/advisories/new) page, or to the maintainer on
GitHub. Reports are kept confidential.
